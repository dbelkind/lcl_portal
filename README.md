# Lifespan Cognition Lab Researcher Portal

A week-by-week calendar of the times lab members can't be in the lab, hosted on GitHub Pages. Members add their unavailable times (classes, other commitments and time off), and any time they haven't added counts as available. Use the arrows or the small month calendar to move between weeks, months and years. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step.

## Using it

Sign in with the Google account a lab manager added. Each person's role is also their place in the Lab Directory: **RAs**, **PhD Students** and **Post-Docs** see only their own schedule, **Lab Managers** (called reviewers in the code) see everyone's and manage the lab, and the **PI** sees their own, like a Post-Doc. RAs' changes to saved times and their time off go to a lab manager for approval; everyone else's save straight away, and lab managers are emailed about PhD Students' and Post-Docs'. The PI isn't a lab manager and is never sent lab management emails, unless they turn on **Lab Management Mode** under **My Settings** (for instance while there's no lab manager): then they can use the site like a lab manager, with every lab management email off until they turn on each kind they want. Lab managers are emailed when the PI turns it on or off, and the email pop-up points out, and leaves unticked, a PI who would be emailed. Someone listed as PI before roles and directory roles were linked becomes the PI, without Lab Management Mode. The top bar has **Schedules** (**Lab Calendar** and **Update Your Availability**), **Lab Directory**, and, for lab managers, **Administrative Schedules** and **Lab Management**, which shows a red badge with how many requests are waiting. The logo and title go back to the Lab Calendar. Dialogs close with their X, Escape, or a click outside them. Under **Request Time Off**, everyone sees their own time off requests and schedule changes, waiting or decided, with who decided and any note; ones decided since they last looked get a red badge (on the tab and the Schedules menu) until they open it. Only lab managers see the email pop-up: anyone else's requests email the lab managers just as the lab managers' own settings say.

- **Class schedule:** pick the semester, then upload a PDF or screenshot, or enter times by hand. The file is read in your browser and is never uploaded. Check the times it found, then save. If your schedule changes partway through, choose **A change to my schedule partway through**: your earlier classes stay, and the new times start on the date you pick.
- **Standing conflict:** regular commitments outside class, which stay on the calendar through holidays and breaks.
- **Request time off:** whole days, or part of a single day, with a required Notes/Reason. For RAs it goes to a reviewer, and shows as pending on your own calendar until it's approved. Editing an approved request sends it back for review.
- **My availability:** edit or remove anything you've saved, and see the status of your requests.
- **My availability** (a tab under Update Your Availability): your own unavailable times as a week calendar, with the list of all your entries underneath. Click your own blocks to change or remove them, drag a block to change its time (drag its top or bottom edge to change when it starts or ends), or drag across empty space to add a time, choosing whether it repeats. A one-off time becomes a time off request.
- **Lab Calendar** (everyone): open participant slots and scheduled visits. Every weekday that isn't a USC staff holiday (including the winter break) gets an open 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM slot, from November 5, 2026, added 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM each morning (at 8:30 AM the lab managers are emailed to book its scan; see **Scan booking reminder** below). Open slots are gray; a booker clicks one to book it, as it is or asking for a start between 8:00 and 10:00 AM, which a lab manager has to approve. Booked visits turn blue and say **BOOKED** until a lab manager confirms them; confirming records the visit's start and scan times and approves or changes any requested start, then reminds them to add the appointment to the lab's Google Calendar with the participant ID and other study details, and offers **Staff this visit**. Confirmed visits show their **lab portion** and **scan** as separate labeled blocks. Lab managers do everything for a visit from here: change an open slot's times before it's booked (or drag it up or down to move the whole visit) (for example after booking the scan at a different time), mark it booked, confirm a booking, staff it, change its times, cancel the booking (opening the slot again), cancel just its lab portion or scan, or cancel the visit. Each visit's dialog shows who's available for each part. Dragging across empty space adds a visit: a full visit, one of three lab-only visits (**Dry & Wet Lab only**, **Phlebotomy & Wet Lab only** or **Wet Lab only**, each needing only those roles; with no phlebotomy, Wet Lab starts within 30 minutes of the start), or a scan only. Confirmed, staffed visits whose lab portion doesn't start at 9:00 AM or whose scan doesn't start at 11:00 AM are marked 🕒 **[SEE TIME]** (each part only for its own start, and only lab managers see it on the site, since only they see staffing). A scan booked for less than 2 hours, such as 1.5 hours, is marked ⚠️ **[SHORT SCAN]** on the scan, and scan staff and lab managers are told in their emails. Days with more than one visit are marked 👥. Staffing details are only shown to lab managers.
- **Administrative Schedules** (reviewers only): **Team Availability**, which switches between everyone's **Unavailable Times** and **Available Times** (each free stretch between 8 AM and 6 PM, for the people you pick: it opens with no one selected, with everyone's name always in view to click on or off. **Select by position or training**, folded away underneath, selects a group at once (RAs, PhD Students, Post-Docs, Lab Managers, PI, and the same training groups as the Availability Checker with their levels inside; ticking a position and a training selects people with both). Switching between Unavailable and Available Times keeps who's selected); **Staffing**, a reference only: every day, weekends and holidays included, has a 9:00–11:00 AM **Lab** slot and an 11:00 AM–1:00 PM **Scan** slot showing who's qualified and free. These aren't visits, so booking and staffing aren't done here: changing a slot's times, deleting one (**Delete**), dragging one or adding one only changes this view, and **Reset** brings back a day's usual slots. A Lab slot needs ( Dry Lab for the whole slot; Phlebotomy for 30 minutes at any point in the slot; Wet Lab for 2 hours starting within 30 minutes after phlebotomy ends, which can run past the slot; Wet Lab and Dry Lab done by different people; phlebotomy goes to the most experienced level free: with no Experienced Phlebotomist the slot is amber with a note that mobile phlebotomy may need to be on stand-by, or booked in advance for difficult veins, and with only a Training Phlebotomist it's red), and a Scan slot needs (two scanners, with Auxiliary Support Scanning paired with Level 2 Scanning, someone with Level 2 Scanning outside weekdays 8 AM–5 PM, and someone for wanding/taping unless that's turned off). A full visit lasts 4 hours from its start; lab staff only come for their own part, and a lab manager finishes the visit after the scan. Lab managers should cover the whole visit between them; if one is only free for the wrap-up the visit is amber, and with none it's red. Red slots can't be fully staffed and say why; amber ones need care. An asterisk marks someone who's only free part of the time. And the **Availability Checker**: for any day, a time range or a specific time, how many in each of five color-coded groups are free (Lab Managers, Phlebotomists, Wet Lab, Dry Lab, and Scanners, which includes Wanding/Taping). Click a group to see who's free by level (and, at a specific time, when busy people are next free). Checkboxes filter the groups, and while a group is ticked its levels get their own checkboxes. A time range can also be tested as a visit.
- **Notes on a visit**: a booker can add a note for the lab managers when booking (with a reminder, and a check before booking, that it mustn't include participant identifiers or a study ID); it's shown to lab managers on the visit and in their booking email, with buttons to copy it into the lab or scan staff's note. Lab managers can add a note for the lab staff and one for the scan staff when confirming or staffing a visit; each goes at the top of that part's LCL Confirmed Visits event. Whether wanding/taping is needed is chosen while staffing: unticking it takes wanding/taping out of the staffing.
- **Staffing a visit** (reviewers only): **Staff this visit** has a drop-down for each role: 📝 Dry Lab, 🩸 Phlebotomy, 🧪 Wet Lab, 🧠 the two scanners, 🧲 Wanding/Taping and 👩‍💻 lab managers (click as many as are handing off). Each lists everyone with the training, free or not; anyone with a conflict is shown in red with what it is and when, and choosing them asks first but can go ahead. Each person shows how many times they've done that kind of role in the 4 weeks up to the visit: Dry Lab and Wet Lab share a count, phlebotomy and lab manager have their own, and scans count once per visit however many scan roles someone has. ⭐ marks the one suggestion for each role: the most experienced phlebotomist free, lab managers, post-docs and grad students before RAs for scans, then whoever has done the role least. The window checks the result as you go. If mobile phlebotomy is booked, tick **Mobile Phlebotomy is booked** instead of choosing a phlebotomist. **Approve staffing** saves it, and the Apps Script puts it on the **LCL Confirmed Visits** calendar. Confirmed visits show **Staffed** on the Lab Calendar once staffed, and the visit's dialog lists any staffing conflict if someone assigned is no longer free. Moving the lab portion moves phlebotomy and Wet Lab with it.
- **Bookings** (under the Lab Calendar, for reviewers and anyone given the **Booking** responsibility under Lab Members): a list. Lab managers see bookings **waiting for confirmation**, **confirmed** ones and **cancelled** ones, and can confirm from there; bookers see the bookings they've made, with their email to the reviewers and **Cancel booking**. Cancelling a booking opens the slot again, emails the lab managers, and doesn't tell its staff. **Participant information never goes on the site**, not even a Screener ID: booking opens an email to the reviewers in the booker's own USC email with the visit's times, any requested start, and a blank Screener ID line, asking them to confirm the booking and record the scan time, staff it, and add it to the lab's Google Calendar with the participant details.
- **My Profile and the Lab Directory**: the first time someone signs in, they fill in their last name, phone number, birthday (month and day, no year), expected graduation (month and year, or N/A for non-students), dietary restrictions/allergies, favorite color and favorite kind of dessert. Anyone missing something added later, such as a last name, is asked again once. They can change it any time under **My Profile** in the menu at the top right, where their email is. **Lab Directory** lists everyone with their directory role (RA, PhD Student, Post-Doc, Lab Manager or PI, set by lab managers on each person's card under Lab members, each with its own color), email and phone number, with tabs along the bottom of the table, like a spreadsheet's sheets, for the whole lab, RAs, PhD Students, Post-Docs, Staff (Lab Managers) and the PI, each with how many people are in it. Click a column heading to sort. A PI's phone number is kept apart from the directory and isn't shown to RAs. Lab managers can switch the table to **Lab Birthdays, Grad Dates, and More**, a sortable table of everyone's birthday, expected graduation, favorites and dietary restrictions/allergies, with the same role tabs, sized to fit the page; nobody else can see it.
- **Account menu** (the avatar at the top right): your email, **My Profile**, **My Settings** (reviewers only) and **Sign Out**.
- **Lab Management** (reviewers only), with **Review Requests**, **Lab Members** and **My Settings** tabs. **Review Requests** (with the same red badge) is split into **Pending**, **Approved** and **Declined**; each decided request says who approved or declined it and when, and the badge updates live as requests come in. **Lab Members** manages who has access, including your own training; **Website Management** is where lab managers edit the USC academic calendar (semesters, with optional finals dates, and holidays, breaks and other dates) and the staff holidays, which the site and the Apps Script use instead of the files in `data/` once saved; **My Settings** has your email alerts. Each tab is also in the **Lab Management** menu in the top bar. Removing someone also deletes their schedule. **Edit Schedule** next to a person opens **Update Your Availability** for them: reviewers can pick anyone under **Entering times for** and add, change or remove their classes, conflicts and time off, which saves straight away without review (time off entered this way counts as approved). Reviewers can also click or drag anyone's blocks on the Unavailability calendar. Each person's training (Wet Lab, Dry Lab, a phlebotomy level of Experienced Phlebotomist, Phlebotomist or Training Phlebotomist, Level 1 Scanning, Level 2 Scanning, Auxiliary Support Scanning, Wanding/Taping, and the Booking responsibility) can be changed at any time under **Lab Members**. Reviewers get emails about requests, cancellations and scans (see **Reviewer emails** below), and choose which under **My Settings**.

Reading works best when each class has its days and times written out as text, such as `MWF 9:00-9:50 AM`. Calendar-style screenshots that show classes only as colored blocks have no times to read, so those need to be entered by hand.

## USC dates

Lab managers keep these up to date under **Lab Management → Website management**. The files below are what the site uses until dates are first saved there.

Semesters, holidays and breaks are in [`data/academic-calendar.json`](data/academic-calendar.json), copied from the [USC academic calendar](https://usc.edu/academic-calendar/) through Summer 2029. Add later years there as USC publishes them. Each "Break between terms" is the gap between official terms, not a name USC uses. Some USC schools publish their own calendars, which can differ.

Class times only appear from the first to the last day of classes, and not on holidays or breaks. Study days and final exams are labeled, but regular class times aren't shown on them.

## USC staff holidays

Participant visits skip the dates in [`data/staff-holidays.json`](data/staff-holidays.json). They follow USC's Paid University Holidays policy (New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas) plus winter recess. A Saturday holiday is observed the Friday before and a Sunday holiday the Monday after. USC sometimes adds a bridge day when a holiday falls on a Tuesday or Thursday, so add those to the file when they're announced. The 2026 winter break runs Dec 17, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027 for staff, matching the student break. Visits still run during student-only breaks.

## How it's set up

This repository is public and holds only the site's code and USC's public dates. Schedules live in a private Firebase database. The site's code runs in people's browsers, so **[`firestore.rules`](firestore.rules) is what actually enforces privacy**. After any change to that file, paste it into the Firebase console (Firestore Database > Rules) and publish it.

The values in `firebase-config.js` identify the project and are meant to be public. Never commit a service account key.

Setup:

1. Create a project in the [Firebase console](https://console.firebase.google.com/) and add a Web app. Copy its values into `firebase-config.js`.
2. Under **Authentication > Sign-in method**, enable **Google**. Under **Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains**, add `dbelkind.github.io`.
3. Create a **Firestore Database** in production mode, and paste in `firestore.rules` under **Rules**.
4. Add the first reviewer by hand: create a collection named `members` and a document whose ID is their Google email in lowercase, with the string fields `name` and `role` set to `reviewer`. Add everyone else from the site.

## Google Calendar and email

[`apps-script/Code.gs`](apps-script/Code.gs) is a Google Apps Script that keeps three calendars in your Google Calendar, covering the next 60 days, and sends the lab's emails. It reads the schedule with your own Google login, so it must run under the account that owns the Firebase project (dbelkind@usc.edu), and it loads the staffing rules from the published site, so push site changes to GitHub before expecting them in the calendars. Its calendar permission only covers calendars the script creates itself, so it can't see or change your other calendars, and it can't share its own.

- **LCL Avail Visits**: one event for each participant visit, for the whole visit. Its color is its booking from November 5, 2026 on: gray when open, blue when booked and waiting to be confirmed, green when confirmed, orange when a booking was cancelled and it's open to rebook (earlier visits keep the calendar's own color). Its title starts ✅, 🟡 or ⚠️ for whether it can be staffed, says why if not, and ends "staffed" once staffing is approved. The description lists who's available for each part and the lab manager, and links to the visit's staffing on the site. It updates every 10 minutes, and straight away after a booking, visit or staffing change on the site (see **Instant updates** below). This was the Lab Staffing calendar, which is renamed rather than replaced.
- **LCL Confirmed Visits**: each confirmed (or staffed) visit, as two events. **Study Visit (names)** covers the whole visit (4 hours from its start) and names the Dry Lab, Phleb and Wet Lab staff, each person once; its description lists who does which ("Wet Lab: Ann", "Phleb: Mobile Phleb"). **Scan Reservation (names)** covers the scan and names the lead scanner, second scanner and wanding/taping, each person once. Titles start with 📝 [SEE NOTE] when there's a note for those staff (the note is then the description's first line, as "*NOTE: …", with a blank line after it), then 🕒 [SEE TIME] for an unusual start and, on the scan, ⚠️ [SHORT SCAN] for a scan under 2 hours. Times, links and the lab manager aren't listed (the lab manager is still invited once staff invites are on). Updated every 10 minutes.
- **LCL Birthdays & Graduations**: everyone's birthday and expected graduation for the coming year, as all-day events with their favorite color, dessert and dietary restrictions/allergies. A graduation is marked on the first of its month, since only the month is known. Updated every hour.

Share all three with each lab manager, and only lab managers: in Google Calendar, open each calendar's **Settings and sharing → Share with specific people** and choose **See all event details**. Bookers don't need them; they use Bookings under the Lab Calendar.

Setup, once:

1. Go to [script.google.com](https://script.google.com) signed in as dbelkind@usc.edu and create a **New project**. Name it "Lab Staffing Sync".
2. Under **Project Settings** (the gear), tick **Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor**.
3. Back in the **Editor**, replace the contents of `appsscript.json` with [`apps-script/appsscript.json`](apps-script/appsscript.json), and the contents of `Code.gs` with [`apps-script/Code.gs`](apps-script/Code.gs). Save.
4. Choose **setUp** in the function menu at the top and click **Run**. Google asks for permission; because it's your own script it may say Google hasn't verified it, so choose **Advanced**, then **Go to Lab Staffing Sync**, and allow.
5. The three calendars appear in Google Calendar within a minute.

Run **setUp** again whenever you paste in a new `Code.gs`. To see what it did, open **Executions** in the Apps Script sidebar. Only events the script made are changed; anything you add to its calendars by hand is left alone. Edit visits on the website, not in Google Calendar, since the next run puts the calendars back in line with the site.

**Updating the calendars by hand.** In the Apps Script editor, choose **syncCalendarsNow** in the function menu and click **Run**: it updates LCL Avail Visits, LCL Confirmed Visits and LCL Birthdays & Graduations straight away, and sends no emails.

**Instant updates.** The site can ask the script to update LCL Avail Visits and send cancellation emails straight away, rather than at the next 10-minute run. Staff invites and emails still wait for that run on purpose, so a mistake fixed in the meantime never reaches anyone. To turn it on: in Apps Script choose **Deploy → New deployment**, pick **Web app**, set **Execute as: Me** and **Who has access: Anyone**, and deploy. Copy the web app URL into `SYNC_URL` in [`firebase-config.js`](firebase-config.js) and push. The site sends it nothing but the request to sync, and the script runs at most once every few seconds, so the link is harmless if anyone else finds it. If USC doesn't allow "Anyone", the calendars simply keep updating every 10 minutes. After changing `Code.gs`, use **Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → New version** so the web app runs the new code.

**Staff emails.** **Switched off for now** (`inviteStaff: false` at the top of `Code.gs`): staff are only listed in the LCL Confirmed Visits descriptions, not added as guests, and nobody but reviewers is emailed. Once it's switched on, lab staff and lab managers are added to the visit event and scanners to the scan event, and every 10 minutes the script emails each person who's affected, and only them, about what changed for them: assigned (with their roles and times), times changed (before and now), taken off, or the visit cancelled, with 🕒 [SEE TIME] when the times aren't the usual ones. Google itself never emails guests. A cancelled booking that's waiting to be rebooked doesn't change anything, so staff only hear about a cancellation once a reviewer cancels the visit on the site. Staff can't turn these emails off. Visits already staffed when staff emails are switched on are never emailed about: the first check after switching on only notes who's staffing what, so only changes from then on are emailed.

**Lab member emails.** **Switched off for now** (`emailLabMembers: false` at the top of `Code.gs`), so only reviewers are emailed. Once it's on, each email goes to one person, so nobody sees who else got it:

- **Class schedules**: a month before spring or fall ends, RAs who'll still be students next semester (going by the expected graduation in their profile) are asked to add next semester's class schedule, with a cheerful note for where the semester is ("Happy (almost) end of the semester!", "Good luck with finals!", "Hope you're having a nice break :)"), then every two weeks until they do or classes start. Anyone not taking classes clicks **I have no classes in [semester]** on the Class schedule tab instead (a reviewer entering times for them can too), which stops the reminders for that semester. Summer is left out (`scheduleSemesters` in `Code.gs`).
- **Finals**: two weeks before finals, RAs still in school are asked to add their exam times, and reminded a week before if they haven't.
- **Request decided**: anyone whose time off request or schedule change was waiting for review hears when it's approved or declined, by whom, with any note.

**Choosing who's emailed.** Before anything on the site that sends an email (sending a request, a Grad Student/Post-Doc saving a change, deciding a request, cancelling a booking or visit, staffing a visit or changing a staffed visit's times), a pop-up lists who'll be emailed. Untick anyone who shouldn't get it, tick **Don't send any email notifications** to send none, or add a message, which is added to the email. **Go back** changes nothing. The choices are saved in `emailChoices` for the Apps Script, which uses each once and deletes it (ones never used are cleared after two days). Emails that are switched off (staff and lab member emails, for now) still show who they'd go to, with a note that nothing will be sent yet.

**How emails look.** Emails go out as HTML with a plain-text copy. Dates and times are bold and highlighted, and key words like **cancelled**, **no longer**, **Before** and **Now** are bold and underlined. Every email ends with "Received this email by mistake? Click here to notify the lab manager if there was an error.", which opens an email to `labManagerEmail` in `Code.gs`.

**Reviewer emails.** Emails go to all the reviewers at once rather than one each, so the account the script runs under doesn't collect copies of every reviewer's email, and nobody is emailed about something they did themselves. Each reviewer picks which kinds they get under **Lab Management → My Settings**; a reviewer who turns a kind off is simply left off those emails.

- **Requests to review**: new time off requests and schedule changes, once per request, linking to **Review Requests**. The first check after setup only notes what's already waiting.
- **Saved schedule changes**: changes and time off Grad Students/Post-Docs saved without review.
- **Cancellations**: straight away when a booking is cancelled (the visit stays open to rebook, and its staff aren't told), or when a booked or staffed visit is cancelled on the site. Visits being booked aren't emailed by the script: the booker emails the reviewers from their own account, with the Screener ID.
- **Scan booking reminder**: at 8:30 AM, which newly opened visit needs its scan booked, with links to the DNI booking page and to the slot on the Lab Calendar, and a reminder to change the slot's 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM scan time there if a different time was booked.
- **Missing class schedules**: a week before a semester starts, which RAs still haven't added their class schedule.
- **Website updates**: from the start of the last semester the academic calendar covers (and the start of the last year of staff holidays), a request to add the next dates under Website management, then monthly until they're in.
- **Birthdays and graduations**: at 8:30 AM, 1 month, 1 week and 1 day before each birthday and expected graduation, and on the morning of, with the person's favorite color, dessert and dietary restrictions/allergies. Nobody is reminded about their own.

## Trying it locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000. If port 8000 is taken, use a different number.

After changing `style.css` or any of the site's `.js` files, raise the `?v=` number on its link in `index.html`, so browsers don't keep using their cached copy.
